5-Note Scale Ladder

Do-re-mi-fa-sol and back down. Climbs a half-step each rep through your whole range. The classic daily warmup.

Full Major Scale

Up and down the full octave scale. Great for evenness between notes once you're warm.

Arpeggio 1-3-5-8

Skipping between chord tones — trains accuracy on jumps, not just steps.

Octave Leaps

Low note, jump the octave, land back down. Builds range and confidence at the top.

Note Match

I play a piano note — you sing it back and hold it. 10 rounds. The purest pitch trainer there is.

Sustain Hold

Hold one note dead steady for 5 seconds. Trains breath support and stops the wobble.

Warmup syllable

Sung on every ladder note. "Mee" for brightness, "Noo/Loo" for smoothness.
